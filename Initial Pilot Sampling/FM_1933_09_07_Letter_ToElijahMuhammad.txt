Central North America
Sept - 7 - 1933, 10 P.M.
Mr. Elijah Mohammed
The Minister of Islam
Detroit, Michigan

Dear Brother

I just received mail from you and Kallatt which brought somewhat happiness in regards to our future labor. Go right ahead with teaching righteousness to our own nation. Allah is with you, stay with in the law of our wholly Quran. Fear not, for the prophet of Allah said HE WHO LEAVES HOME IN SEARCH OF KNOWLEDGE WALKS IN THE PATH OF ALLAH; Read your Quran, outline your subject and be well posted before giving life to the dead Nation of Islam in the wilderness of North America. Accept no bribe, friendship or apples of no kind. Your happiness is awaiting you on completion of your labor. I have hard labor to go through with misunderstanding on the part of my peoples in this part of the planet. However, I have treasured all my life love for the lost-found Nation of Islam and the time will prove all things that are righteousness must be revealed and come to an understanding of all mankind. NOW BROTHER I can see you when you read this letter, don’t cry but think of the future and steady your nerve. Study your lessons, the wonders you are doing which is measured and it MAKE THEM ALL OF THE WEALTH IN THE WORLD. FROM YOUR BROTHER - W.D. Fard

# Diagnostic Framing in the Nation of Islam, 1952–1977: A Collective Action Frame Analysis Under Perspectival Critical Realism

## Corpus Note

This analysis draws on documents held in the `1952-1977 - Contestation/` directory of the project corpus, selected for centrality to the Elijah Muhammad–Malcolm X contestation and the 1975 Warith Deen Muhammad succession, and for comparability with the Period 1 (1930–1951) analysis. The full selection of up to fifty sources is listed in the **Works Cited** at the end of this document; sources are cited in-text by short title. Louis Farrakhan's 1978–1982 restorationist speeches lie outside this evidentiary window and are not analyzed here except where a 1964–1975 document by "Louis X" (his name in this period) falls inside it. FBI files are used only where they independently corroborate dates, organizational facts, or the state's own diagnostic framing of the movement (Frame D5, below); they are not used to adjudicate contested historical facts the corpus itself cannot settle.

This revision draws substantially deeper on the `Elijah Muhammad/Official_Publications` subfolder — the 1960–1975 run of *Muhammad Speaks* itself — since the newspaper, published under his own Mosque No. 2 and carrying both his signed columns ("Teachings of the Holy Quran") and the standing "What the Muslims Believe"/"What the Muslims Want" program printed in nearly every issue, is the movement's primary weekly organ of record and the main vehicle, alongside his books, through which his own words reached the general membership. Fourteen of the fifty cited sources are drawn directly from this run, selected across 1960–1974 for legibility, doctrinal significance, and comparability with the corresponding Period 1 material.

This document is the **diagnostic** volume of a three-part frame analysis (Diagnostic / Prognostic / Motivational), expanding and superseding the diagnostic material previously compressed into a single combined report. It follows the analytical structure of the Period 1 diagnostic analysis so the two periods can be read side by side.

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: Ranked Diagnostic Frames

1. **D1 — The White Man as Devil by Nature (Yacub's History)** — the foundational cosmological diagnosis, stable across Elijah Muhammad's entire twenty-two-year sampled corpus (1957–1974).
2. **D2 — Racial-Political Institutional Exclusion ("Victims of Americanism")** — an institutional, non-cosmological diagnosis foregrounded by Malcolm X from March 1964, present but subordinated in Elijah Muhammad's corpus throughout.
3. **D3 — Mental Slavery: Christian and Educational False Consciousness** — the corpus's most temporally continuous diagnosis, present in structurally parallel form on every side of every schism examined.
4. **D4 — The Hypocrite/Defector Diagnosis** — a diagnosis of internal, not external, threat: the claim that a movement figure's public dissent is caused by moral corruption, jealousy, or "hypocrisy" rather than by any defect in the movement's own teaching.
5. **D5 — State Surveillance and Government Conspiracy** — two related but distinct diagnoses: Malcolm X's diagnosis of an active "government conspiracy" (not merely private bigotry) obstructing Black political power, and the independently documented fact that the FBI's own files diagnosed the movement itself, in the state's classificatory language, as a "Potential Threat to Internal Security."
6. **D6 — The Grafted/Colored Mind as a Transferable Psychological Structure** — Warith Deen Muhammad's 1975 doctrinal revision of D1: the "devil" is reinterpreted from a fixed hereditary biological category into a "colored," artificial mentality that can inhabit a Black body, which both preserves and quietly dismantles the founder's racial ontology at the exact moment of succession.

These six frames are not evenly distributed across the period. D1 and D3 are the load-bearing frames of Elijah Muhammad's stable middle period (1957–1974); D2, D4, and D5 cluster overwhelmingly in the eighteen months of acute contestation (March 1964–February 1965); and D6 belongs almost entirely to the ten months following Elijah Muhammad's death (February–December 1975). Read together, they show a diagnostic architecture that is simultaneously the most doctrinally stable and the most repeatedly, specifically contested feature of the whole corpus.

## DETAILED DIAGNOSTIC FRAME ANALYSIS

### D1 — The White Man as Devil by Nature (Yacub's History)

**Definition.** The condition of Black subordination is diagnosed as the deliberate, and ongoing, product of a genetically engineered "devil" race — the outcome of the rebel scientist Yacub's ancient eugenics program — who rules not by superior merit but by "tricknology." The category "white" is not a social position but a created biological-moral kind.

**Core Diagnosis.** The cause of Black suffering is *ontological*, not circumstantial. This converts what could be read as a political-economic grievance (unequal treatment, exploitation, discrimination) into a cosmological one: the antagonist's nature, not merely his conduct, is the problem, which is precisely what forecloses reform as a possible remedy (see Prognostic Frame P1).

**Problem Representation.** The corpus frames the injury as a hidden truth actively concealed from the "so-called Negro" by the very devil-class that benefits from the concealment — hence the persistent phrase "so-called Negro" itself, which diagnoses a *naming* problem (a false identity imposed from outside) prior to, and generative of, every subsequent material problem.

**Causal Explanation and Representative Quotations.**
- "In order to make the American so-called Negroes worship the devils, the American devils paint Jesus, God and the Angels white... so-called Negroes have worshipped the devils (white race) all of their lives" (*The True History of Jesus' Birth, Death, and What It Means to You and Me*, 1957).
- "We cannot hope for justice from the devils when by nature there [is none]... devils have you afraid and worshipping that which you know not" (*Our Saviour Has Arrived*, 1974).
- "The devil certainly did a 100 per cent job in turning the people against" their own nature and food (*How to Eat to Live*, Book 1, 1967) — the diagnosis extends from theology and history into the believer's own body and diet, a continuity with the Period 1 corpus's "poison food" diagnosis (Beynon, 1938), now recast in explicitly biological-racial terms rather than merely dietary ones.
- Elijah Muhammad's response to Malcolm X's 1964 departure restates the doctrine without engaging any of Malcolm X's stated reasons for leaving: "There is much talk... about the ending of the world that we now live in (the world of the white man). The white race is losing their divine power to rule" (*Muhammad Speaks*, Sept. 11, 1964) — evidence that, under strain, the frame's response to external shock is restatement, not revision.
- Louis X's December 1964 attack on Malcolm X likewise assumes, rather than argues, D1 as settled doctrine, treating any departure from it as personal betrayal rather than reasoned disagreement (*Muhammad Speaks*, Dec. 4, 1964; see D4, below).
- Elijah Muhammad's own regular *Muhammad Speaks* column, "Teachings of the Holy Quran," restates the Yacub etiology in its most explicitly causal form five years later, glossed directly onto Qur'anic verse: "MR. YAKUB'S made-man (white race) is the people that started fighting to appear among us... The white race is the real cause of it," immediately followed by his own gloss on Qur'an 7:27 ("Surely We have made the devils to be the friends of those who believe not"): "This happened to the Black People who followed Yakub from Arabia. He pulled off, from their original self, the clothes of Righteousness... You are warned. You believe you are safe in following the invitation to evil and filth of the white race. Wait and see" (*Muhammad Speaks*, July 4, 1969) — the same column runs, in the identical issue, under the sub-heading "The Disgrace," pairing the Yacub history directly with Qur'an 5:51's warning against taking "the Jews and the Christians for friends," fusing D1 with a scriptural-exclusivist register absent from the 1957 books' more purely historical-narrative presentation.
- The "We Must Have Freedom, Justice and Equality" campaign that runs across *Muhammad Speaks*' first several volumes (1960–1963) is diagnostically significant precisely for what it withholds: front-page copy reading "Would you like remaining a permanent slave or being a permanent member of a soup line?" (Vol. 1, No. 2, Dec. 1961; Vol. 2, No. 25, Aug. 1963) names *economic* deprivation but frames its cause, in the same issues' inside pages, as an inherent property of "the So-Called Negro['s]" slave-master relationship rather than as a correctable policy failure — a diagnostic move consistent with, and preparatory to, D1's ontological register even where the surface language ("profiteers," not "devils") briefly narrows the object of blame, as in the same period's headline "Blame Profiteers, Not All Whites, For Negro..." (Vol. 2, No. 25, Aug. 1963) — a rare, if qualified, softening of D1's collective-hereditary attribution that the corpus does not sustain or develop further in the sampled issues.

**Responsible Agents.** Yacub as originating scientist; by extension the entire "Caucasian" population as his genetically continuous, morally culpable descendants — a doctrine of collective, hereditary responsibility rather than individual culpability, structurally identical to the Period 1 corpus's *Supreme Wisdom Lessons* etiology of the "grafted" race.

**Affected Population.** The "twenty-two million ex-slaves" / "so-called Negroes" of Elijah Muhammad's own preferred numerical formula — a population understood as the "Lost Sheep," "Lost Tribes," or "Lost People of God" temporarily resident in "the wilderness of North America."

**Connection to Prognostic Frames.** Because the diagnosis is ontological, only exit — territorial, economic, and social separation (P1) — is represented as a logically available remedy; reform of a devil-by-nature relationship is not offered as a coherent option anywhere in Elijah Muhammad's sampled corpus.

**Connection to Motivational Frames.** Feeds directly into prophetic/eschatological urgency (M1) and cosmic status-reversal (M4): if the antagonist's nature is fixed and cosmological, urgency and reward are keyed to prophetic time, not political contingency.

#### PCR Analysis
- **Empirical/Discursive Level.** Carried by pseudo-scientific numeracy (brain-ounce comparisons, exact dates — "Expired in Nineteen and Fourteen," inherited directly from the Period 1 *Supreme Wisdom Lessons*), by biblical grafting and serpent imagery, and by an explicit vocabulary of concealment ("so-called," "tricknology," "the devil's hiding place" — the latter recurring, transformed, in D6 below).
- **Actual Level.** The frame's observable work across five books spanning seventeen years (1957–1974) is to relocate the source of Black subordination from a contingent, renegotiable historical relationship to an immutable created nature — a move that is stable regardless of whether every member held the cosmology as literal biology.
- **Real Level (Retroduction).** *Analytical inference, not direct proof:* the frame's unmodified endurance across five separately published books and a direct doctrinal response to the 1964 schism is consistent with (not conclusive proof of) a generative mechanism in which the frame's organizational utility — as an unfalsifiable, non-negotiable boundary marker separating movement insiders from a hostile outside world — outweighed pressure toward doctrinal accommodation. The corpus contains no direct testimony from Elijah Muhammad explaining the frame's function *as* boundary-maintenance; this is offered as a plausible mechanism, not an established fact.

**Relative Importance:** **High** — the deepest and most temporally stable diagnostic frame in the corpus, and the single frame from which both Malcolm X (1964) and Warith Deen Muhammad (1975) independently, and separately, depart.

**Evidence Across Corpus:** TSWV (1957); TTHOJ (1957); MTTBIA (1965); HTETL Books 1–2 (1967, 1972); TFOA (1973); OSHA (1974); EM's Sept. 1964 response to Malcolm X's departure; Louis X's Dec. 1964 "Biggest Hypocrite" article (by presupposition); *Muhammad Speaks*, Vol. 1, No. 2 (Dec. 1961) and Vol. 2, No. 25 (Aug. 1963), "We Must Have Freedom, Justice and Equality" campaign; *Muhammad Speaks*, Vol. 8, No. 42 (July 4, 1969), "Teachings of the Holy Quran"/"The Disgrace" column. Contrast: absent from Malcolm X's post-March-1964 corpus and explicitly historically bounded, though not repudiated outright, by Warith Deen Muhammad in 1975 (see D6).

### D2 — Racial-Political Institutional Exclusion ("Victims of Americanism")

**Definition.** The condition of Black Americans is diagnosed as the product of ongoing, *identifiable* political mechanisms — voter suppression, gerrymandering, Dixiecrat control of Congress, government-tolerated economic exploitation — rather than, or in Elijah Muhammad's corpus in addition to, a fixed cosmological order.

**Core Diagnosis.** Malcolm X's April 1964 "The Ballot or the Bullet" diagnoses the problem in *institutional*, not ontological, terms, naming specific Senate committee structures, specific legislators ("Richard Russell," "Eastland"), and specific electoral mechanics (seniority rules, gerrymandering, the Dixiecrat wing of the Democratic Party).

**Problem Representation.** "I'm one of the 22 million black people who are the victims of Americanism... victims of democracy, nothing but disguised hypocrisy" — the problem is a systemic failure of a *stated* democratic principle, not a fixed metaphysical order. Malcolm X sharpens the same diagnosis on March 12, 1964, in his statement announcing the Muslim Mosque, Inc.: "22 million of our people who are still here in America need better food, clothing, housing, education and jobs right now" — a diagnosis of urgent, present material deprivation running alongside, rather than replacing, the long-range separatist diagnosis he still professed at that date.

**Representative Quotations.**
- "The only reason the Dixiecrats control these committees is because they have seniority. The only reason they have seniority is because they come from states where Negroes can't vote" (Malcolm X, "The Ballot or the Bullet," April 3, 1964).
- "You and I in America are faced not with a segregationist conspiracy, we're faced with a government conspiracy" (same speech) — this line is diagnostically load-bearing enough to warrant its own frame (D5, below), since it names the *state itself*, not merely private prejudice, as the causal agent.
- "It isn't that time is running out — time has run out!" (same speech) — a diagnostic claim about the closing of a historical window, feeding the distinct urgency register of Motivational Frame M5.
- Elijah Muhammad's own texts also invoke this register: "Separation of the so-called Negroes from their slave-master's children is a MUST" is preceded, in the same book, by an extended argument about compounded, uncompensated slave labor (*Message to the Blackman in America*, 1965) — showing the political-economic diagnosis is present in Elijah Muhammad's corpus too, but subordinated to, and ultimately explained by, the cosmological diagnosis (D1).

**Responsible Agents.** Named legislators and committee structures; the Democratic Party as an institution tolerating its "Dixiecrat" wing; more diffusely, "the government" as such, rather than "the white man" as a racial-biological category — a marked shift in the unit of analysis from D1.

**Connection to Prognostic Frames.** Because this diagnosis is compatible with multiple, competing prognoses — electoral mobilization (P2), armed self-defense (P5), or international human-rights appeal (P3) — it becomes the single diagnostic frame around which the sharpest prognostic contestation in the corpus unfolds after March 1964 (see Prognostic volume).

**Connection to Motivational Frames.** Underwrites a *this-worldly*, conditional urgency (M5) sharply distinct from D1's prophetic/eschatological urgency (M1): the ballot-or-bullet framing makes urgency contingent on whether existing democratic channels are permitted to function, not on a fixed prophetic date.

#### PCR Analysis
- **Empirical/Discursive Level.** Directly stated with unusual institutional specificity: named senators, named committees, vote counts, and a literal map of Negro population density displayed in the U.S. Senate chamber, which Malcolm X cites as having personally observed a week before the speech.
- **Actual Level.** The frame does the work of making Black political action legible as a *rights claim within* the American constitutional order ("The Constitution of the United States of America clearly affirms the right of every American citizen to bear arms," invoked as a citizen's right) — a markedly different operation from D1, which places its audience *outside* that order altogether ("I don't even consider myself an American," the same speech, only sentences earlier — a documented internal tension, not a resolved position).
- **Real Level (Retroduction).** *Retroductive inference:* the co-presence, in the same April 1964 speech, of "I don't even consider myself an American" and an appeal to the Constitution as the source of a right to armed self-defense is consistent with a diagnosis actively being reworked, rather than a stable synthesis. The corpus does not permit certainty about which element Malcolm X regarded as primary at that specific date; this report flags the tension as evidence of instability rather than resolving it.

**Relative Importance:** **High** for the post-March-1964 period specifically; present but consistently subordinate in Elijah Muhammad's corpus throughout the full period.

**Evidence Across Corpus:** MX_1964_03_12_DeclarationOfIndependence; MX_1964_04_03_Speech_BallotOrBullet; MTTBIA (1965, subordinated register); MX_1964_06_28_Speech_OAAUFoundingRally (reformulated, see D5/P3).

### D3 — Mental Slavery: Christian and Educational False Consciousness

**Definition.** The condition of Black subordination is diagnosed, in significant part, as a problem of *internalized belief* — a "mental" or "spiritual" captivity produced by Christian religious formation and by white-authored public education — prior to, and enabling, material subordination.

**Core Diagnosis.** "The Bible is the graveyard of my poor people (the so-called Negroes)" (TSWV, 1957) diagnoses the problem as epistemic: the wrong sacred text and the wrong self-concept produce active complicity in one's own subordination. Warith Deen Muhammad's 1975 restatement makes the mechanism unusually explicit: "The Church told us that we didn't have any opportunity in this world but that our hope was in a world to come... The Church never did tell us that we had an opportunity right then to come into heaven spiritually" ("Artificial Blackness," *Muhammad Speaks*, June 27, 1975) — the same diagnosis as TSWV eighteen years earlier, restated in a fully doctrinal register even as its author was simultaneously dismantling D1 (see D6).

**Problem Representation.** Malcolm X extends precisely this diagnosis into secular education: "Our children are being criminally shortchanged in the public school system... The textbooks tell our children nothing about the great contributions of Afro-Americans" (OAAU Founding Rally, June 1964) — a structurally identical diagnosis (false knowledge produces false self-conception) applied to a different institution.

**Representative Quotations.**
- "The Bible, church and Christianity have deceived the so-called Negroes" (TSWV, 1957).
- "Every little child going to school thinks his grandfather was a cotton picker. Why, your grandfather was Nat Turner... your grandfather was Hannibal" (Malcolm X, OAAU Founding Rally, 1964).
- "We can never grow into the true Knowledge of Self until we learn and understand the history of ourselves. Our pitiful condition as a people in the world today is a result of the brutal butchering of our original mind (mentality) by those who ripped us from our native soil. They skillfully severed from our minds those functions of mental and spiritual development which classify a physical body as 'man' or 'human'" (W. D. Muhammad, "Artificial Blackness," June 27, 1975) — nearly two decades after TSWV, the diagnosis of engineered mental captivity is not merely retained but intensified and given a more explicitly psychological vocabulary.
- W. D. Muhammad, in his first official interview (March 21, 1975), retrospectively diagnoses the same phenomenon among Christian Black Americans of the founding period: "black people were almost 100% Christian... You didn't have to go to Church to be born with a Christian mind" — treating pre-NOI Black Christian consciousness itself as the object requiring correction, a claim structurally continuous with the Period 1 corpus's own diagnosis of Christianity as "tricknollogy."

**Connection to Prognostic Frames.** Licenses an educational/pedagogical prognosis distinct from either separation (P1) or electoral politics (P2): the Nation of Islam's own school system (documented in *Muhammad Speaks*), the OAAU's proposed community-controlled schools, and Warith Deen Muhammad's later doctrinal "renewal" of religious instruction all follow from D3 rather than from D1 or D2 alone.

**Connection to Motivational Frames.** Feeds a distinct motivational register — not urgency toward an external enemy but urgency toward self-transformation and re-education, later echoed in the "Knowledge of Self" catechism W. D. Muhammad restates as late as August 1975 ("Who Is The Original Man?").

#### PCR Analysis
- **Empirical/Discursive Level.** Directly and repeatedly stated across Elijah Muhammad's corpus (1957–1974) and independently, in structurally parallel form, in both Malcolm X's 1964 OAAU material and W. D. Muhammad's 1975 material — three independent lines of the same diagnostic operation.
- **Actual Level.** The frame does the work of making leadership itself indispensable: if the primary problem is false consciousness, only an authoritative corrective teacher — not merely a policy change — can resolve it, which underwrites the intense personalization of authority visible throughout the corpus, on every side of every schism.
- **Real Level (Retroduction).** *Retroductive inference:* the frame's independent emergence in Elijah Muhammad's religious register, Malcolm X's later secular-educational register, and W. D. Muhammad's 1975 psychological register suggests a shared underlying logic of "corrective pedagogy against internalized subordination" that outlasted the personal schism between the men — i.e., the schism operated far more at the level of prognosis and loyalty (Motivational Frame M2) than at the level of this diagnostic structure, which every faction continued to deploy.

**Relative Importance:** **Medium-High.** Present throughout the whole 1952–1977 period, but functions more as a supporting diagnosis beneath D1 (Elijah Muhammad's corpus), D2 (Malcolm X's later corpus), or D6 (W. D. Muhammad's corpus) than as a freestanding master diagnosis.

**Evidence Across Corpus:** TSWV (1957); MX_1964_06_28_Speech_OAAUFoundingRally; IWDM_1975_03_21_Interview_FirstInterview; IWDM_1975_06_27_MS_Article_ArtificialBlackness; IWDM_1975_08_22_MS_Article_WhoIsTheOriginalMan.

### D4 — The Hypocrite/Defector Diagnosis

**Definition.** A diagnosis distinct from D1–D3 in that its object is *internal* rather than external: the claim that a movement figure's public departure and dissent is caused by personal moral corruption — jealousy, ambition, "hypocrisy" — rather than by any defect in the movement's teaching, thereby diagnosing the *problem* as the defector, not as anything the defector might have said.

**Core Diagnosis.** Louis X's December 1964 article does not engage any of the substance of Malcolm X's post-departure positions (D2, the Mecca reversal of D1, or P3); it diagnoses the departure itself as evidence of a pre-existing character flaw: Malcolm X is "defector and hypocrite Malcolm," whose "shameful attempts... to slander and destroy the great Islamic leader" had "caused concern not only among the ranks of Muslims, but among many fair-minded non-Muslims" (*Muhammad Speaks*, Dec. 4, 1964).

**Problem Representation.** The category of "hypocrite" (borrowed, notably, from the same Quranic vocabulary used in the *Supreme Wisdom Lessons*' "Rebels against the Will of Allah" in the Period 1 corpus) forecloses argument: a hypocrite's stated reasons for dissent are, by definition of the category, pretextual, so there is no diagnostic content in Malcolm X's post-1964 statements that requires a substantive answer.

**Representative Quotations.**
- "Such a man as Malcolm is made... Worthy of death, and would have met with death if it had not been for Muhammad's confidence in Allah for victory over the enemies" (Louis X, Dec. 1964) — language published roughly ten weeks before Malcolm X's assassination on February 21, 1965.
- Elijah Muhammad's own September 1964 restatement of doctrine, published in direct response to Malcolm X's departure, performs a related but milder version of the same diagnostic move: rather than naming Malcolm X, it reasserts, at length and without concession, the very doctrines Malcolm X had just publicly begun to depart from — diagnosing the departure, by omission, as simply mistaken rather than as raising any question the doctrine itself must answer.

**Responsible Agents.** The individual defector, understood as personally culpable — a notably different attribution of agency than D1 (hereditary, collective) or D2 (institutional, structural).

**Affected Population.** The movement's general membership, whose loyalty is the object being defended, and secondarily the "fair-minded non-Muslims" the article claims were also alarmed.

**Connection to Prognostic Frames.** Functions to discredit, by association with the discredited person, any prognosis (P2, P3, P5) Malcolm X subsequently proposed, independent of that prognosis's substantive content — i.e., D4 is a diagnostic move whose entire purpose is prognostic pre-emption.

**Connection to Motivational Frames.** D4 is the diagnostic content underneath Motivational Frame M2 (loyalty-and-betrayal urgency): the two are close to inseparable in the corpus, but are analytically distinguished here because D4 answers "what is wrong" (a corrupted individual) while M2 answers "why must you act now" (to defend the movement's legitimacy against that individual).

#### PCR Analysis
- **Empirical/Discursive Level.** Directly documented in Louis X's December 1964 article, including the explicit "worthy of death" language.
- **Actual Level.** This is a point requiring particular evidentiary caution. The corpus contains a documented published statement describing Malcolm X as "worthy of death" prior to his assassination. **This is direct textual evidence of the existence and publication of such language.** It is **not**, on the strength of this corpus alone, evidence of a causal link between this specific publication and the assassination itself, which involved separate individuals, a criminal investigation, and a historical record outside the scope of the sampled documents. This report records the frame's documented content and timing; it does not draw a causal inference the corpus cannot support.
- **Real Level (Retroduction).** *Retroductive inference, clearly bounded:* the intensity and personalization of this diagnosis in late 1964 is consistent with a generative mechanism in which an authority structure built substantially on personalized charismatic and prophetic frames (D1, M1) had few available registers for absorbing public, doctrinally grounded dissent from a former senior minister other than total moral delegitimization of the dissenter. This is offered strictly as one plausible mechanism explaining the frame's severity, not as an account of any specific actor's intent regarding the events of February 1965.

**Relative Importance:** **High** specifically at the moment of schism (1964); the frame recedes, without being formally repudiated, under the reintegrative approach of 1975 ("the past is the past... we will not dig it up," W. D. Muhammad).

**Evidence Across Corpus:** LF_1964_12_04_Article_BiggestHypocrite; EM_1964_09_11_Article_ResponseToMXLeaving.

### D5 — State Surveillance and Government Conspiracy

**Definition.** Two related but distinct diagnostic operations, both documented directly in the corpus: (a) Malcolm X's diagnosis that the government *itself*, not merely private segregationist sentiment, is the active agent obstructing Black political power; and (b) the independently attested fact that the FBI's own files diagnosed the Nation of Islam and Malcolm X, in the state's own classificatory language, as a "Cult" exhibiting "Racial Hatred" and "Advocacy of Violence," filed under the heading "Potential Threat to Internal Security."

**Core Diagnosis.** Malcolm X: "You and I in America are faced not with a segregationist conspiracy, we're faced with a government conspiracy" ("The Ballot or the Bullet," April 1964) — the diagnostic object is upgraded from individual racist actors to the structure of government itself. Independently, and from the opposite direction, the FBI's 1955 file table of contents diagnoses the movement's very existence — its doctrine, leadership, and growth — as itself the problem to be tracked and contained, under headings including "Racial Hatred," "Advocacy of Violence," and "Subversive Tendencies."

**Problem Representation.** Malcolm X's February 14, 1965 speech, delivered the night after his own home was firebombed, sharpens this diagnosis into a direct claim of organized, unresolved threat to his life and his family, distinguishing it in register (personal, immediate, embodied) from the more institutional language of "The Ballot or the Bullet" a year earlier: "I was in a house last night that was bombed, my own... I don't normally come out in front of people without a shirt and a tie... but I was caught in what I had on" (Ford Auditorium, Detroit, Feb. 14, 1965).

**Representative Quotations.**
- "The only reason the Dixiecrats control these committees is because they have seniority" — read alongside the FBI files' own documented surveillance activity beginning years before the 1964 schism, this establishes that state attention to the movement long predates, and is causally independent of, the internal Elijah Muhammad–Malcolm X contestation.
- FBI file section headings (1955): "Racial Hatred," "Advocacy of Violence," "Subversive Tendencies," "Potential Threat to Internal Security" — this is directly evidenced by the file's own table of contents and body text, not an inference about the file's content.
- Malcolm X, describing the night of the firebombing: "It didn't destroy all my clothes, not all, but you know what happens when fire dashes through — they get smoky... in trying to get my family out of the house, none of us stopped for any clothes at that point — twenty-degree cold" (Ford Auditorium, Feb. 14, 1965).

**Responsible Agents.** For Malcolm X's diagnosis: elected officials, committee structures, and "the government" as an institution. For the FBI files themselves: the state security apparatus as an independent third framing actor, operating with its own diagnostic categories entirely apart from, and prior to, either the Elijah Muhammad or the Malcolm X camp's internal dispute.

**Connection to Prognostic Frames.** Malcolm X's government-conspiracy diagnosis licenses armed self-defense (P5) and international human-rights appeal (P3) as remedies precisely because domestic constitutional channels are diagnosed as captured; the state's own surveillance diagnosis, by contrast, generates no prognosis *within* the movement's corpus — it is a rival framing project entirely external to the movement's own frame-making, which the movement's texts do not directly answer or even, in most instances, acknowledge by name.

**Connection to Motivational Frames.** Underwrites both this-worldly conditional urgency (M5) and, in the specific case of the February 1965 speeches, a motivational register of personal courage under direct, embodied threat (M6).

#### PCR Analysis
- **Empirical/Discursive Level.** Malcolm X's claim is stated in specific institutional vocabulary (committees, seniority, gerrymandering); the FBI's diagnosis is stated in the state's own bureaucratic-security vocabulary (section headings, classification labels), which this report treats as directly evidenced textual fact, not editorial characterization.
- **Actual Level.** The FBI files independently corroborate that sustained federal surveillance of the movement (Fard, 1957; the Nation of Islam, 1955 and 1957; Malcolm X, from at least 1953 across at least five field-office jurisdictions — Headquarters, New York, Washington, Chicago, and Phoenix) predates the 1964 schism by years, meaning the movement's own diagnoses of persecution and "government conspiracy" were not solely rhetorical constructs responding to the schism but tracked a documented, independently existing state security interest.
- **Real Level (Retroduction).** *Retroductive inference, explicitly bounded:* the corpus does not permit confident inference about the causal effect of this state surveillance on the movement's internal dynamics — for instance, whether surveillance pressure contributed to the intensified internal loyalty-policing documented in D4. This report flags that boundary explicitly rather than crossing it; the two diagnostic operations (Malcolm X's political diagnosis and the FBI's own security diagnosis) are presented here as parallel, independently documented facts, not as a demonstrated causal chain.

**Relative Importance:** **High** for the specific 1964–1965 period; the FBI material is best read as establishing an evidentiary floor and external context for the whole corpus rather than as itself a frame *deployed by* the movement.

**Evidence Across Corpus:** MX_1964_04_03_Speech_BallotOrBullet; MX_1965_02_14_Speech_AfterFirebombing_FordAuditorium; FBI_1955_Files_NOI; FBI_1957_Fies_NOI; FBI_1957_Files_FardMuhammad; FBI_1953-1971_Files_MalcolmX_HQ; FBI_1953-1971_Files_MalcolmX_NewYork; FBI_1958-1971_Files_MalcolmX_Chicago.

### D6 — The Grafted/Colored Mind as a Transferable Psychological Structure

**Definition.** Warith Deen Muhammad's 1975 doctrinal revision of D1: rather than a fixed hereditary biological category, "the devil" is reinterpreted as a "colored," artificial *mentality* — a "germ" of corrupted mind — capable of inhabiting any body, including a Black one, which preserves the founder's vocabulary while quietly converting a racial-essentialist diagnosis into a psychological one.

**Core Diagnosis.** "We now have you in our hands in your black body but bearing the white man's mind in your being... If we would wipe all grafted devils out with a physical destruction, we would still have them on hand in your black body" ("The Death of White Supremacy," *Muhammad Speaks*, July 18, 1975). This sentence does real diagnostic work distinct from D1: it explicitly denies that physical, racial destruction of "devils" would solve the problem, because the problem is now located in a transferable mental structure rather than in heredity as such.

**Problem Representation.** "Who is the original man? ... The original is that which has not been dealt with artificially, taken out of its original form, and put into grafted, artificial form" ("Who Is The Original Man?", Aug. 22, 1975). W. D. Muhammad simultaneously retains the founder's vocabulary of "grafted," "germ," and "Caucasian mentality," and redefines "white man" as anyone who carries a "colored mind" — including, by his own explicit statement, Black people themselves: "We call the Caucasian white supremacist the colored man because his mind is colored... Nobody knew anything about racism until the colored man from Europe came up with the idea of white supremacy."

**Representative Quotations.**
- "This world, the world of grafted Caucasian mentality, has grown old... It has grown so hopeless today until people are just satisfied to go to work, return home, get some sleep, wake up, and then repeat that cycle over and over again until they die" ("The Death of White Supremacy," July 18, 1975) — the diagnosis of civilizational exhaustion is retained from Elijah Muhammad's eschatology (compare D1/M1's "expired in 1914"), but its object — a "mentality," not a bloodline — has shifted.
- "The devil, who is capable of the most beautiful speech, uses that speech to attract the weak people so he can rule over them as a beast. The history of the Church is the history of a beast" (same document) — D3's Christianity-as-deception diagnosis is retained essentially unchanged even as D1 is being reworked around it, direct evidence that the 1975 transformation is selective, not wholesale.
- "The past is the past, unless it is dug up. We will not dig it up" — W. D. Muhammad's own explicit framing of his relationship to the founder's racial teaching, which sets D1 aside as belonging to a completed historical phase of the mission rather than arguing against it point by point.

**Responsible Agents.** "Grafted Caucasian mentality" as a free-floating psychological contagion — "you eat it with your eyes; you eat it with your ears; you eat it with your mind when you're not even thinking" — rather than a fixed population defined by descent, which is the decisive diagnostic break from D1.

**Affected Population.** Explicitly widened beyond the founder's racial categories: "the germ of the devil-being first existed in a black body," meaning the diagnosis's origin point is relocated even further than a simple color-reversal of D1 would suggest.

**Connection to Prognostic Frames.** Licenses reintegration and universalization (P4) rather than separation (P1), because if the antagonist is a transferable mentality rather than a fixed race, exit from all white persons is no longer the only coherent remedy; correct knowledge and "renewed mind" become sufficient.

**Connection to Motivational Frames.** Replaces M1's eschatological racial urgency with an urgency of psychological self-correction ("Bring Your Mind In Full Form"), continuous with D3 but now explicitly untethered from D1's racial ontology.

#### PCR Analysis
- **Empirical/Discursive Level.** Carried through a deliberate, extensive retention of the founder's exact vocabulary ("grafted," "Caucasian mentality," "germ," "the Body-Christ") redeployed with a changed referent — evidence of "interpretive succession" rather than declared doctrinal rupture, exactly as W. D. Muhammad describes his own role: a "Mujeddid," "one to watch over the new Islam... to see that it is constantly and continually being renewed."
- **Actual Level.** The frame functions to reposition the movement for external legitimacy without requiring W. D. Muhammad to publicly repudiate his father's founding doctrine — a documented external effect (per contemporaneous press coverage) is the movement's rapid, concrete shift toward normalized relations with civic and financial institutions within months of succession.
- **Real Level (Retroduction).** *Retroductive inference:* the near-immediate character of this reframing, following directly upon succession, is consistent with a generative mechanism in which succession crisis and institutional-financial pressure (the movement's substantial documented property holdings at the point of transition, detailed in "Accomplishments of the Muslims," 1975) created incentive toward a legitimation strategy compatible with mainstream institutions, while the "germ"/"mentality" vocabulary allowed this shift to be presented as doctrinal deepening rather than doctrinal abandonment. The corpus does not contain internal deliberative documents that would raise this above inference.

**Relative Importance:** **High**, and represents the corpus's second and final instance of full diagnostic transformation by a principal leader (after Malcolm X, 1964) — but, unlike 1964, achieved through authorized succession rather than departure, and therefore without triggering an equivalent to the D4 "hypocrite" delegitimization response.

## COMPARATIVE NOTE: Continuity and Change from Period 1

Frame D3 (mental slavery via religious and educational false consciousness) is the corpus's most direct continuation of a Period 1 frame: Fard's and Elijah Muhammad's 1930s diagnosis of Christianity as "tricknollogy" designed to keep believers "IGNORANT" (*Supreme Wisdom Lessons*; *Final Call to Islam*, 1934) reappears almost unchanged in register across 1957, 1964, and 1975 sources, on every side of every later schism. Frame D1 is the direct descendant of the Period 1 Yacub etiology, retained at full doctrinal strength for the entirety of Elijah Muhammad's leadership and independently abandoned, at different moments and for different reasons, by both Malcolm X and Warith Deen Muhammad. Frames D2, D4, and D5, by contrast, have no clear equivalent in the Period 1 corpus, which shows comparatively little institutional-electoral diagnosis (D2), no documented internal "hypocrite" schism-diagnosis of comparable intensity (D4, though the *Supreme Wisdom Lessons*' "Rebels against the Will of Allah" is a structural precursor), and a lighter, less legally documented state-security presence (D5) than the post-1953 FBI files provide for Period 2. This suggests the 1952–1977 period's diagnostic architecture is best read as the Period 1 cosmology under sustained external pressure (legal, political, generational) that it did not yet face at the movement's founding.

The False Light Of Christianity

When they got material wealth, they started oppressing others instead of carrying the light of truth to the poor and ignorant masses who were in darkness. Instead of the liberating light of truth, they carried slavery to those who were in darkness. They chained us and kept us back from education and real scriptural "meat" (divine truth).

The Christian leadership gave the general society that accepted their rule, scriptural drunkenness. They gave them a lot of spirit but little or no vision or reasoning, which made the masses of our people drunk in religion. Most of their churches have offered the world nothing but darkness and silence, although they have been making a lot of noise and shining a lot of false light. In some of the churches the people are taught to kneel before statues. This is nothing but paganism and it turns the people into idol worshippers. The way of life for many so-called "pagan" or "primitive" societies is much more decent than the way of life that "Christians" have established for us in this society. 

Some of the things that are accepted and not even questioned in this society are looked down on by the "primitive" people of the earth. This society will conduct shameful acts and feed no shame. The priest, the preachers and the rabbis will not come before the public and condemn the society and tell the people to make this country right or else they will be the enemy of the church. They will not break friendship with the government in order to correct the wrongs in the society. They tolerate these evils because they are there to make a business for the religious leaders and not to make a world for Almighty God.


"Divine" Racism

When they left Europe and came to the Western hemisphere, they saw a need to wipe out the Indians, so they used "Christianity" to justify the wicked position that they took. They have preached wickedness and enslavement to the people of color the world over. As long as a preacher holds up a cross with a Caucasian man on it and tells the people that this is the manifestation of God in the flesh, he is promoting white supremacy and keeping it in existence. 

They say, "It doesn't matter what flesh God manifested himself in, aren't we all one humanity?" Those are the words of a shrewd devil trying to blind us to the evils that they want to put over on us. If God was to pick a particular flesh (physical) man to represent Himself as the real manifestation of the person of God on earth, that would be "divine" racism. God Did Not Die On The Cross

The Bible never says that the Jews as a flesh people are the favored people of God. Only as long as they follow in the spirit of God are they worthy to be called the "children of God." There are many sections of the scripture that show us how God took His blessings away from one of a certain family and put them on another one of another family. Jesus did not say anything in his teachings to contradict these things in the scripture. Jesus spoke of himself as a common person and a human being, and he said that he did not know everything. He said that he could not tell the people what would happen tomorrow because only God had that knowledge. When Jesus was dying on the cross, as the Christian book says, he said, "My God, My God, why hast thou forsaken me?" This lets you know that God was not dying on the cross, Jesus was dying on the cross. The Christian church takes the physical body that was not God and puts it up before you as God in the flesh. They did not teach our mothers and fathers to pray to Almighty God, the Creator of the heavens and the earth, they taught them to pray to Jesus. Jesus never told anybody to call on him. "Christianity" Held Us In Check

The Caucasian society encouraged us to be Christians because "Christianity" was keeping us in the corral. The church's teaching has been nothing but a corral for the work animals of America. They designed that corral and used all the teachings that they could possibly teach us out of context to keep us as work animals. They told us not to hate those that hate us, and that we should turn the other cheek when we are struck on one cheek. We are to pray for those who spitefully use us; if our enemy takes our coat, we are to give him our hat; and if he asks us to walk one mile we are to walk two miles. This is what was fed into our fathers' and mothers' minds and hearts by the church preacher to keep us quiet, submissive and obedient to wicked, cruel leadership. The outside society wondered why the Bilalian (black) community did not rise up by the thousands when the Caucasians would take one of our innocent brothers and lynch him, or when they would set his body afire and burn him alive while they drank beer and had a party before the burning corpse.

The Cross: A Sign Of Death

Our slave grandparents used to sing a song about the cross in which they called it an emblem of suffering, shame and death. They said that some day they hoped to exchange the old rugged cross for a starry crown. They did not want the cross because they understood that it was a burden and an evil thing for Jesus. Jesus, in heaven, would not have the cross but he would have a starry crown. If the cross is something that we hope to get rid of, why cherish it? Another powerful evil of the cross is that, along with its white supremacist powers, it is also the symbol of death. 

The cross makes people who love the cross lose respect for life. Because they cherish the sign of death and worship a god that is victorious in the grave, they began to subconsciously look to the grave for victory. They even wait until they reach the grave to repent and stop doing their evils. When they feel death coming upon them, they call a priest or a preacher and they want to confess their sins. The cross makes you respect death, but it makes you disrespect life and hate life.

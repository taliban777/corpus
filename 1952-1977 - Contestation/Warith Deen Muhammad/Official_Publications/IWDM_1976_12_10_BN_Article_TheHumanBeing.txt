In the Name of Allah, the Beneficent, the Merciful; Peace and Blessings upon His Servant and His Messenger, Muhammad, forever. Amen.

There is no god but Allah; Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah.

As -Salaam –Alaikum

My Dear Brothers and Sisters,
Almighty God does not want the human being to remain solely on a physical flesh level, He wants him to come alive as a mental and a spiritual being and grow into a totally balanced person. Allah has put this capacity in the human being, and he has the potential to grow along the natural dimensions of human development that God intended for his man (mind) in order that he may reach the proper balance.

The deceivers come along and they reverse God's way; they tell the people savage tales that have no base in reality. They twist the picture language of God into some fairy tale language, separating it from its base in reality. The deceivers tell the people that God is not talking about their sinful flesh, the sinful creation and this material world. They tell the people that when God talks about the marvelous creation of which man as a flesh being is only the beginning, He is talking about something that has its reality in the sky. They tell the people that the Creator is not talking about something that has its reality on the earth. These enemies to truth tell the people that demons are in the heart of the earth and that this whole physical world is a hellish world. The deceivers tell the people that the surface of the earth itself is torment, and that when they die, their souls are going to sink beneath the surface into the interior of the earth. They tell them that Satan will be waiting with a pitch fork to take their souls, and throw them into the open furnace of fire.

The enemies of truth want you to believe that there in the interior of the earth Satan is going to torture you forever for lying, for committing fleshly fornication and fleshly adultery, and for stealing a loaf of bread. The wicked people who do not want you to accept reality and truth tell you that your soul will be condemned to hell for not confessing Jesus Christ as God in the flesh and for not confessing that Jesus was the only begotten son of God. They tell the people that if they do not confess belief that Jesus died for them and that his precious, holy, innocent blood paid for their sins that they will go down in the heart of the earth into the fiery furnace to be tortured by Satan forever, even if they have not lied, stolen or committed fleshly fornication or fleshly adultery. The people accept these lies in their minds, they give their bodies and their souls to the preacher in the church and they go to their death believing in fairy tales.

Brothers and sisters, we must attack the lies that have been given to Christians that should not be on Christians, and we must attack the lies that have been charged to Jesus, who is innocent of all of those lies. We should ask the question, "Why does Imam W.D. Muhammad constantly force us to get back into our holy image?" The answer is simple. We must be determined to keep our hands on the handle of truth and to keep our eyes looking at God, the Author of Truth. We must remain an orderly congregation and we must have leadership in an orderly society. We must have self government in the individual and we must respect government in the society and in the world. We continue to teach you these things like all of the Servants of Allah have done because there is a precious creation that God has made for human beings to come into. That creation is the human essence, the human substance, the human nature, the human mind, the human soul and the human spirit. Those persons who do not want to come into this nature and who do not want to be true human beings decided long ago that they were going to keep human beings from coming into power on earth.

The human being stood up and he tried to fight the battle against falsehood and lies alone, but he could not defeat them because the deceivers had gotten the jump on the human being. They were greedy for the physical world so they got the physical materials before the human beings could get strong on the earth. The deceivers came into material power and they used the superiority of physical weapons to keep the good people and the
innocent people under their feet. The people prayed out to God to show them the way out of the prison cell that these materialistic monsters had put them in, and they asked Him for a way out of the hell of moral corruption that the wicked sinners had put them in. The Holy Quran says that God answered the prayer of the righteous caller. The Book says that God told the angels (the forces in creation), even before He made the human being, that He was going to make a ruler in the earth. God told the angels, "When I have breathed into that stinky, muddy - shaped, human being of My own knowledge and wisdom, bow down, you angels, to My new creation."

Brothers and sisters, when we talk about the man (mind) that God wanted to make to be ruler in the earth, we are not talking about the fairy tale or kindergarten concept of man that you have seen pictured in Bibles and sculptures of religious figures. We are talking about something greater than you in your own physical description, greater than the whole humanity in physical description and greater than anything that you can put in physical description. This is because just one mind and just one heart made in the image of God's ruler is greater than all of the other human beings, although they may number billions or more on the earth. We are talking about a particular (a special) kind of mind, heart, nature and spirit that God wanted to make to rule the earth, and which all of us have the capacity to become, if we will open up our minds to truth and allow God's words to take form in us.

Thank you for honoring us with your time to read these few words.

Your brother, W.D. Muhammad

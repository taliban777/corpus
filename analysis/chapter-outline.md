# Chapter Outline
## Divergent Framing Projects After Elijah Muhammad, 1978–1995

### Provisional chapter thesis

After the 1975 succession crisis, Louis Farrakhan and Warith Deen Mohammed articulated divergent leadership projects through different configurations of diagnosis, prognosis, and motivation. Their rhetoric shared resources inherited from the Nation of Islam and Black liberation traditions, but assigned different meanings to religious identity, organisational continuity, collective boundaries, and legitimate action.
